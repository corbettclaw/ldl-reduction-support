Swarm City Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Swarm City is published by Christine Moran. Contact us at corbettclaw+ldlapp@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Rehearsal and data on your iPad

Rehearsal is scripted and makes no API calls. Swarm City saves your city tiles, mission, history, shared discussion, and the latest 500 activity entries in app storage on your iPad. It saves your selected model, individual agent prompts, and review pace in app preferences. A separate local usage ledger stores request times, selected model IDs, token counts, and reported USD charges. It remains after Reset city so weekly totals include previous cities. Settings can fetch OpenRouter’s public model catalog without sending your API key, mission, or city. If you provide an OpenRouter API key, the app stores it in the device Keychain. Swarm City has no developer-operated backend, advertising, tracking, or app analytics code.

Optional Live mode

Live mode requires your own OpenRouter API key and a compatible model. After you confirm each run, the app sends your key to OpenRouter and sends your mission, individual agent instructions, city tiles, and recent shared discussion to OpenRouter for routing to the selected model provider. OpenRouter and the provider process those inputs and the generated responses to provide the Live feature. The key is not sent to the model provider by the app.

OpenRouter and model providers have their own data handling practices, which can depend on your account settings and selected model. Read OpenRouter’s privacy policy and the selected provider’s policy before using Live. Do not enter sensitive personal information in the mission, agent instructions, or city discussion.

Sharing and deleting your data

Export creates a JSON file containing the city, mission, history, discussion, and recent activity (including instructions captured for Live runs). It excludes the saved API key, but the text may contain information you entered; review the file before sharing it. Reset city replaces the saved city, history, discussion, and activity. Delete key separately removes the Keychain credential; use that control before removing the app. Removing the app removes its local app storage, but iOS may retain Keychain items for a later reinstall. To manage data held by OpenRouter or a model provider, use the controls and contact methods in their policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or third-party service behavior changes. The effective date above will change with the update.
